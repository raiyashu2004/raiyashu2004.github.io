DealFlow360

An intelligent, self-governing sales operations platform engineered for complex quote-to-cash workflows, multi-depot inventory fulfillment splitting, cycle-anchored billing proration, and dual-surface RBAC.

GitHub Repository → Explore in Doors

How It Started & The Problem

Enterprise B2B sales cycles regularly collapse at the transition between quoting, inventory reservation, and invoicing. Sales representatives often bypass discount limits, warehouses suffer stock contention across concurrent deals, and billing teams calculate proration credits manually across hybrid one-time and recurring subscriptions.

Engineered for the Odoo Hackathon Finale 2026, DealFlow360 automates the entire quote-to-cash lifecycle into a self-governing, deterministic state machine. Built with a bespoke React 18 + Vite SPA (featuring custom SVG charts and zero external UI libraries), an Express 5 backend, and a 24-table PostgreSQL relational engine, the platform enforces hard ACID guardrails on pricing, discount tier escalations, warehouse splits, and commission settlement.

Key Technical Innovations & Engineering Highlights

1. Blended Risk & Hierarchical Escalation Routing

Rather than simple flat discount limits, DealFlow360 calculates cumulative blended margin risk across an entire quotation. Smaller overages spread across many lines compound into a risk index, preventing sales reps from "giving away an order" by keeping each line marginally beneath ceilings. Quotes exceeding thresholds automatically route up hierarchical approval chains (Sales Rep → Sales Manager → Finance Officer).

2. Greedy Multi-Warehouse Splitting with Stock Exclusivity

Inventory fulfillment operates strictly on uncommitted stock (`on hand − units allocated to confirmed orders`). The engine evaluates multi-depot inventory with a greedy optimization algorithm: reusing existing shipping origins first, then picking the depot with highest remaining volume, and falling back to the lowest freight rate depot for remainder backorders. Restock runs trigger automated consolidation alerts.

3. Dual-Surface Isolation & 102 End-to-End Automated Tests

The system implements two strictly isolated authentication surfaces: `df_session` for internal staff and `df_portal` for customer quotation approvals, with cross-tenant isolation enforced at the database query level. The entire platform is backed by a 102-check end-to-end automated test suite over HTTP verifying every edge case, from cycle proration to automatic backorder restocking.
